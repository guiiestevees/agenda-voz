Privacy policy

Updated November 3, 2026

Summary

Tap Bee has no account or login and shows no ads. Your appointments, habits, points and preferences are kept on your own device.

What stays on your device

  • Appointments, notes, habits, Pomodoro sessions, points, titles and check-in streak.
  • Your name or nickname, if you choose to provide it.
  • Preferences such as app color and appearance.

This data is not sent to any server of ours. In the iPhone app, a copy is kept in your own iCloud, on your Apple account, so it comes back if you change devices or reinstall the app. We have no access to it.

When you speak or write to Bee, your secretary

To understand your request, the app sends our server the sentence you said, the current date and time and a summary of your calendar for the coming weeks (name, day, time and repetition of appointments), plus the text of your undated notes. The server passes this on to Anthropic, the company that provides the artificial intelligence (Claude), and returns the answer to the app.

To prevent abuse, the server counts how many requests each device and internet address (IP) made that day, using a random number that does not identify you. We do not keep these sentences or your calendar. Anthropic handles the data under its own privacy policy and does not use it to train models.

Turning your voice into text is done by the speech recognition of your iPhone or browser (Apple, Google, etc.), under their own privacy rules.

Invitations

When someone uses an invite code, the server keeps only the code and how many people used it, to award points to whoever sent the invitation. To prevent the same device from counting several times, the internet address (IP) stays linked to the code for up to 1 year. We do not keep names, e-mail addresses or phone numbers.

Onboarding questionnaire

To improve the app, we count how many people reach each screen of the onboarding questionnaire. It is only a total per screen: your answers, your name and your device number are not sent, and this count does not keep your internet address.

Subscription

Purchases are made through Apple, and we do not receive your card details. To confirm whether a subscription is active, we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous code generated by the app and the purchase history, such as product, date, price and country. It does not receive your calendar or your name.

Reminders, microphone and photos

The app asks permission to use the microphone (to hear you) and to show notifications (reminders). Story cards are created on the device itself and only leave it if you choose to share them.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you are in full control: you can edit or delete any appointment in the app, or delete everything by removing the app or the site data. Privacy questions can be sent to alquimiadigital08@gmail.com.
