How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Choose Tap Bee and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles billing and refund requests. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple account and choose the purchase.

I changed iPhones or reinstalled the app. What now?

On the payment screen, tap Restore purchase, using the same Apple account as your subscription. Your appointments are kept in your iCloud and come back when you open the app with that account.

How do the two taps on the back of the iPhone work?

The app guides you through setup after you subscribe. The steps are also available in Settings, inside the app. Once set up, tap twice on the back of your iPhone to talk to Bee.
